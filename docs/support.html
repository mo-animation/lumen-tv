Lumen Player Support

Lumen Player is a media player for your own playlists. It does not include or sell any channels, movies or series.

How do I add my playlist?

On the first screen, enter your Xtream Codes server address with your username and password. For an M3U playlist, paste its link into the first field and leave the other two empty.

Can I try Lumen Player without a playlist?

Yes. Choose Try the demo on the first screen, or use our sample playlist of open movies and public test streams: mo-animation.github.io/lumen-tv/qa.m3u

A channel or video won't play

Check that it plays with your provider's own app. For live channels, try Settings → Live stream format and switch between HLS and MPEG-TS.

How do I switch provider?

Settings → Sign out, then enter the new details.
